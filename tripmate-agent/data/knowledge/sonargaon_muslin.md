# Sonargaon and the muslin trade

Historical writing about Sonargaon describes the town and its surrounding area as a centre for making and exporting fine cotton muslin. This textile trade was part of the region's historic commercial importance.

## Verified facts

- Sonargaon and its neighbourhood were historically known for making and exporting fine cotton muslin.
