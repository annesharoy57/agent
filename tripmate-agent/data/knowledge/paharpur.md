# Somapura Mahavihara at Paharpur

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, “Ruins of the Buddhist Vihara at
Paharpur.” This note paraphrases the UNESCO description and Outstanding
Universal Value; the source description is available under CC-BY-SA IGO 3.0.

Somapura Mahavihara, also called the Great Monastery, is near Paharpur in
Naogaon District in northwest Bangladesh. UNESCO describes it as evidence of
the growth of Mahayana Buddhism in Bengal from the 7th century and as an
intellectual centre until the 12th century.

The monastery's layout was designed around its religious function. UNESCO
highlights its harmonious plan and carved decoration as an important artistic
achievement that influenced Buddhist architecture as far away as Cambodia.
The site is therefore relevant to visitors interested in Buddhist heritage,
archaeology, and historic architecture.
