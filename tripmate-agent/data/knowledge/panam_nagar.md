# Panam Nagar: the merchant town

Panam Nagar is a historic commercial and residential area within Sonargaon. Its surviving townscape developed in the nineteenth century and flourished into the first half of the twentieth century, during British colonial rule. Panam is a later merchant district within the wider historic region, not the intact medieval capital of Isa Khan.

## Verified facts

- Panam Nagar's surviving merchant townscape developed in the nineteenth century and flourished into the first half of the twentieth century, during British colonial rule.
- Panam Nagar is a later commercial district; it is distinct from the older political history of medieval Sonargaon associated with Isa Khan.
