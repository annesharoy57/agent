# The Sundarbans

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, “The Sundarbans.” This note is a short
paraphrase of the UNESCO description and Outstanding Universal Value; the
source description is available under CC-BY-SA IGO 3.0.

The Bangladesh World Heritage property is in the southwest of the country,
adjoining the Bay of Bengal. UNESCO describes a tidal mangrove landscape with
waterways, mudflats, and small forested islands. The wider Sundarbans mangrove
region lies in the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Meghna delta.

UNESCO identifies the area’s mangrove biodiversity as a major reason for its
importance. Its description names the Royal Bengal tiger, estuarine crocodile,
and Indian python; its Outstanding Universal Value also discusses dolphins and
other threatened species. Do not treat this list as a promise of wildlife
sightings.
