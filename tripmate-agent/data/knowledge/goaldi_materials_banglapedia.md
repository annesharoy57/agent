# Goaldi Mosque and Bengal's local building materials

Goaldi Mosque is part of Bengal's Sultanate-era mosque tradition. Stone was not locally available in much of Bengal, while clay was abundant in the delta. Builders could make bricks from that clay, so brick became a traditional building material. Terracotta decoration was also made from clay. Bengal's brick-and-terracotta style reflects these local materials and established building traditions.

This does not mean stone was never used. Goaldi Mosque's central mihrab has carved dark stone decoration, while its side mihrabs use brick and terracotta.
