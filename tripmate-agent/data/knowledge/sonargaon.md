# Sonargaon: history and highlights

Sonargaon, in Narayanganj, was an important political centre in eastern Bengal. A Government of Bangladesh environmental assessment describes it as a capital of Bengal and an administrative headquarters of eastern Bengal at different times between 1335 and 1535.

## Verified facts

- At different times between 1335 and 1535, Sonargaon served as a capital of Bengal or the administrative headquarters of eastern Bengal.
- The Moroccan traveller Ibn Battuta visited Sonargaon in 1346.
- Nearby Goaldi Mosque dates to 1519, during the Sultanate period.
