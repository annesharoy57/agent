# Sonargaon and Panam Nagar's later history

Source: Journal of Jagannath University, “Conservation Status of Panam City: Challenges and Prospects.” Panam Nagar is a later merchant township within the wider Sonargaon heritage area, not the same thing as Sonargaon's medieval Sultanate capital. The study dates Panam's growth to the nineteenth century and says it continued to flourish until the end of the Second World War.

This later colonial-era history should be kept distinct from Sonargaon's earlier political role as a capital and administrative centre in medieval Bengal.
