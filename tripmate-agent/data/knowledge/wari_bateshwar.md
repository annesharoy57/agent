# Wari-Bateshwar, a separate archaeological site

Source: Banglapedia, “Wari-Bateshwar,” published by the Asiatic Society of Bangladesh.

Wari-Bateshwar is a separate archaeological site in Narsingdi District, Bangladesh. Banglapedia describes a fortified city there as about 2,500 years old. This date and fortified-city description refer to Wari-Bateshwar, not to Sonargaon.
