# Bengal's clay, bricks, and terracotta

In his overview of Bengal architecture, John Burton-Page notes that stone in the Bengal region was concentrated around the Rajmahal basalt hills, while fine alluvial clay was freely available. This clay supplied the material for characteristic Bengali bricks and terracotta. The account describes Bengal mosques that combined a brick core with selective stonework and terracotta decoration.
