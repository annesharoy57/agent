# Historic Mosque City of Bagerhat

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, “Historic Mosque City of Bagerhat.”
This note paraphrases the UNESCO description and Outstanding Universal Value;
the source description is available under CC-BY-SA IGO 3.0.

The historic mosque city is in southwest Bangladesh, in the Bagerhat area.
UNESCO says it was founded in the 15th century by Ulugh Khan Jahan and was
formerly known as Khalifatabad. The site is notable for early Islamic
architecture in Bengal, including mosques and other monuments, many built of
brick.

UNESCO describes an historic urban landscape that also includes infrastructure
such as roads, bridges, water tanks, and reservoirs. Shait Gumbad Mosque and
Khan Jahan's tomb are among the monuments identified in the UNESCO account.
These heritage features make the site relevant to visitors interested in
architecture and history.
